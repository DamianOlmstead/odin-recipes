Description

Onigiri is a rice snack filled with your choice of filling and wrapped in nori(seaweed).

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Tuna
  • Mayo
  • Nori

Steps

  1. Cook Rice
  2. Prepare Filling
  3. Lay out half the rice in a triangle shape
  4. Put your filling ontop of the rice
  5. Put the rest of the rice ontop of th filling
  6. Wrap in Nori
  7. Enjoy!
